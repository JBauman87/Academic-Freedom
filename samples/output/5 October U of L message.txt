University of Lethbridge statement on 
suspension of Dr. Anthony Hall
WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 5, 2016 
As has been publically noted by Dr. Anthony Hall and subsequently reported by media outlets, 
the University of Lethbridge seeks to clarify that on October 4, 2016 Dr. Hall was suspended 
without pay by the University of Lethbridge pending an internal review into possible violations 
of the Alberta Human Rights Act. The University has suspended Dr. Hall in accordance with the 
Post-secondary Learning Act of the Province of Alberta.
The University has taken immediate and active steps to ensure that the suspension of Dr. Hall 
will not impact or disrupt the studies of the affected students of the University of Lethbridge.
As there is an internal review currently underway, the University will not provide any additional 
commentary at this time.
